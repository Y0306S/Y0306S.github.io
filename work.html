GEM-MM

project · vision-language alignment

An on-policy preference method for VLMs: score chain-of-thought with a fork-versus-final entropy reward, then update the full model with group-normalised policy gradients (SEGA).

  • On a small budget of 3,000 MM-RLHF prompt pairs, standard multimodal DPO-style, image-conditional, and self-improvement pair methods saturate.
  • On Qwen3-VL-4B, GEM-MM beat those alignment baselines under the same small budget (AMBER Hallucination, HallusionBench).
