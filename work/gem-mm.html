GEM-MM

project · vision-language alignment

The problem

Preference-aligning a vision-language model on a small budget is easy to saturate. With only 3,000 MM-RLHF prompt pairs, standard multimodal DPO-style, image-conditional, and self-improvement pair methods stop improving.

What I did

  • Built an on-policy preference method that scores chain-of-thought with a fork-versus-final entropy reward.
  • Updated the full VLM with group-normalised policy gradients (SEGA).
  • Compared against those saturating pair methods on Qwen3-VL-4B.

What came out

Under that small training budget, GEM-MM beat the SOTA alignment baselines on AMBER Hallucination and HallusionBench.
