Self-evolving hyperparameter search

Data and Intelligence Research Lab, Peking University · research assistant · Dec 2025 – Oct 2026

The problem

Hyperparameter Optimization (HPO) itself is a very big problem: especially in high dimensional spaces or finding more efficient ways (less compute or less time). Current research in using LLMs for Automated Machine Learning (LLM4AutoML) is still in its earlier stages where LLMs are used as a warm-start to traditional Bayesian optimisation algorithms. But we thought what if involving LLMs in the so called 'improvement phase' could help improve the performance of HPO as compared to traditional methods?

And with the integrations of LLMs, the fact is that it can be trained on previous results or runs, can we possibly create a self-evolving HPO system that can learn from previous runs and improve itself?

What came out

On the lab’s HPO setup, the post-training stack beat SOTA Bayesian methods and SOTA LLMs by 27%.
