Self-evolving hyperparameter search

DAIR Lab, Peking University · research assistant · Dec 2025 – Oct 2026

The problem

Hyperparameter search is expensive if every new task starts from scratch. The question was whether a finished tuning job on a similar task is actually worth transferring, and how an LLM sits next to Bayesian optimisation instead of replacing it blindly.

What I did

  • Built a tuning framework that mixes Bayesian optimisation with open-source LLMs.
  • Used a Gaussian process to find similar tasks, and fANOVA to decide if a finished job can transfer.
  • Designed a multi-stage post-training stack on Qwen-3.5-9B (SFT, DPO, REINFORCE).
  • Started extending the same transfer idea to peptide and query optimisation by encoding a combinatorial search space with a VAE into a continuous latent space.

What came out

On the lab’s HPO setup, the post-training stack beat SOTA Bayesian methods and SOTA LLMs by 27%.
